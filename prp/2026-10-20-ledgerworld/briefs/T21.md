## Specification
- Reverse stress: search over the six families' parameters for the smallest shock that breaches the reserve floor under the selected policy; returns the failure path and a sensitivity table; its search cohort is never mixed into representative estimates. One family at a time on a baseline: an 8-point monotonicity grid, then an exact integer binary search on a rules-replay world (unmatched observations → `no_action`, flagged), then one fresh branch confirms; no probability field, every result labelled a search.
- Collateral: borrowing base = Σ eligible collateral × (1 − haircut), concentration limits; simulation only, never wired to execution. Haircuts round down; one pass of limits on the pre-limit total (obligor, then asset type); unknown, stale and pledged lots add nothing.
- SHOULD, not MUST: if time runs out, this task is the cut (system brief §15).
