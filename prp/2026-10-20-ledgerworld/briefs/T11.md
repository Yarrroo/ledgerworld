## Specification
- Policies: no-action, fixed reserve, integrated (draws an eligible advance, defers what is contractually deferrable, converts just in time). Objective lexicographic per §9: obligations met → reserve floor kept → funding cost.
- Paired evaluation: every policy sees the same seeds and branches; agents re-react independently per policy (fresh branch per policy). In a policy branch the policy drives the treasury (C1 `params.source = policy:<id>`) and the treasury model is not called; feasibility and cost come from the branch's realised ledger; the key is integer and summed, never averaged.
- The selected policy decides again on the current observed snapshot (`plan_for_now`) to produce the legs the execution module binds; a decisions result is never an approval.
- Nonanticipativity: a policy's decision at node t may read only information observable at t; a test policy that reads a future receipt is rejected with the offending field named. Two guards: a read-only view whose leaves carry `available_at`, and a paired-history check (identical history through t → identical decision at t) that catches a side channel.
- When every candidate is infeasible: return the earliest unmet obligation, the binding constraint and who to escalate to; never relax the reserve floor silently.
- Register the test ids in checks/required.json under g1.
